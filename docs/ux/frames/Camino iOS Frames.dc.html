1wReading guide, motion, Reduce Motion, VoiceOver, notification, icon. The written half of the spec.
THREE SIGNALS, THREE CHANNELS
Distance (house) — house width and how far down the path it sits. Changes only on protocol save. Never moves back on rescue.
Brightness (sky) — height of the horizon glow band, number of visible stars, and the sky ramp. Driven by actual mg over 7 days.
Weather — cloud mass above the horizon plus a diagonal rain hatch over the whole frame. Driven by rescue mg and count. It is texture, so it survives greyscale, Increase Contrast, and a bright room.
MOTION
Idle: cloud drift ~40 s cycle, glow breathing ±3% over 12 s. No walk cycle, no particles at 60 fps. Confirm: the stone drops 3 pt and loses its lit edge over 220 ms; light haptic. Save protocol: the house eases to its new size and y over 900 ms, once. Rescue: cloud opacity crossfades up over 1.2 s; no haptic, no flash. Arrival: night to held day over 2.5 s, once; light haptic.
REDUCE MOTION (V5)
No drift, no breathing, no house travel, no sheet matched-geometry. Every scene change is a 250 ms crossfade between two static paintings. Sheets use the standard system presentation. Arrival crossfades; it does not pan.
VOICEOVER (X5)
Scene: “Road toward home. House far. Sky dark. Weather clear.” Step: “22:00, 0.125 milligrams, not confirmed.” Taken: “22:00, took 0.125 milligrams.” Less: “22:00, promised 0.125, took 0.0625.” Skipped: “22:00, skipped.” Off night: “No dose on the path tonight.” Controls: “Rescue”, “Promise”, “Look back”, “2 nights still open”. Chart bars: “week of 15 July: promised 0.75 mg, taken 0.875 mg, rescue 0.125 mg.”
